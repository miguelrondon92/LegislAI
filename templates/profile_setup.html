          How are these preferences used?
          • Bills are analyzed by AI to identify their policy categories and impacts
          • Your preferences are compared against bill categories to generate alignment scores
          • Alignment scores range from -100% (strong conflict) to +100% (strong alignment)
          • You'll receive alerts for bills that strongly align with or conflict with your preferences
          • These preferences are stored locally and can be updated anytime
